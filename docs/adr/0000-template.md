# ADR-NNNN: Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the situation that demanded a decision? List forces, constraints,
related decisions. Cite earlier ADRs by number when relevant.

## Decision

State what we're doing in a single, declarative sentence. Then expand
with the substance of the design.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why rejected.
- **Option B** — why rejected.
- **Option C** — why rejected (or why it's a future migration target).

## Consequences

Positive and negative. Include the obligations this decision creates
(things future contributors must keep doing) and the foreclosures
(things this decision now prevents without a follow-up ADR).
